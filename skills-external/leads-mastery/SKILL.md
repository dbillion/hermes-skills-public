# Leads Mastery: Chapter-by-Chapter

This skill implements the "Core Four" and "Lead Magnet" frameworks to create a surplus of engaged leads.

## Section I: Start Here

### Chapter 1: The Problem This Book Solves
*   **Core Concept:** Doubling leads effectively doubles the business. Ignorance of your offer is the primary constraint.
*   **Metric:** "Double leads = Double business."

## Section II: Get Understanding

### Chapter 2: Leads Alone Aren’t Enough
*   **Core Concept:** A "lead" is a contact; an "engaged lead" is a hand-raiser.
*   **Execution Checklist:**
    - [ ] Deploy an engagement filter (e.g., a question or reply request).

### Chapter 3: Engage Your Leads (Lead Magnets)
*   **Core Concept:** The "Problem-Solution Cycle." A lead magnet solves a narrow problem and reveals the next problem your core offer solves.
*   **Execution Checklist:**
    - [ ] Select a narrow, meaningful problem.
    - [ ] Delivery Method: Software, Info, Service, or Physical.
*   **Quote:** "Give Away The Secrets, Sell The Implementation."

## Section III: The Core Four (Get Leads)

### Chapter 4: #1 Warm Outreach
*   **Core Concept:** Reaching out 1-to-1 to people who know you.
*   **Execution Checklist:**
    - [ ] Aggressively list all contacts.
    - [ ] Use the **A-C-A framework** (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask).
*   **Metric:** "100 reach outs per day."

### Chapter 5: #2 Post Free Content
*   **Core Concept:** 1-to-many outreach via the Hook-Retain-Reward structure.
*   **Execution Checklist:**
    - [ ] Topic sourcing from "Far Past," "Recent Past," or "Manufactured."
    - [ ] Apply "Give-to-Ask" ratios (Give 10: Ask 1).

### Chapter 6: #3 Cold Outreach
*   **Core Concept:** 1-to-1 to strangers. Requires "Big Fast Value" to overcome lack of trust.
*   **Execution Checklist:**
    - [ ] Personalize initial contact within 30 seconds of attention.

### Chapter 7: #4 Paid Ads
*   **Core Concept:** 1-to-many outreach by buying attention.
*   **Execution Checklist:**
    - [ ] Focus on the "Reason Why" hook.

## Section IV: Lead Getters (Scale)

### Chapter 8: Referrals
*   **Core Concept:** Getting customers to bring more customers.

### Chapter 9: Employees
*   **Core Concept:** Scaling via Document, Demonstrate, Duplicate (3Ds).
*   **Execution Checklist:**
    - [ ] Document the lead-getting checklist.

### Chapter 10: Agencies
*   **Core Concept:** Renting an expert's system until you can build your own.

### Chapter 11: Affiliates
*   **Core Concept:** Incentivizing 3rd parties to promote your offer.
