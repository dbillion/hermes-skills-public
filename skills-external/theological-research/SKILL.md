# Theological Research Skill

Use this skill to research, structure, and format theological papers according to seminary standards.

## Workflow

1. **Thesis Formation**: Clearly define the research question.
2. **Source Exegesis**: Gather 5+ sources (3+ printed).
3. **Drafting**: Use the 80/20 rule (80% own words, 20% expert quotes).
4. **Referencing**: Strictly follow APA 7.
5. **Styling**: Balance rigor with personal, conversational tone.
