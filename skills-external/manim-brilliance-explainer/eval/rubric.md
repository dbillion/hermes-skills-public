# Rubric

Score each category from 1 to 5.

| Category | Question |
|---|---|
| Insight | Does the animation reveal the core idea? |
| Continuity | Are objects transformed rather than replaced? |
| Technique | Is the right technique used for the job? |
| Cognitive load | Is the screen uncluttered? |
| Pacing | Is the timing comfortable? |
| Aesthetic | Is the visual style clean and focused? |
| Correctness | Is the math accurate? |
| Story | Is there a clear hook and payoff? |
| Camera | Is every camera move meaningful? |

A strong scene should score at least 4 in every category.
