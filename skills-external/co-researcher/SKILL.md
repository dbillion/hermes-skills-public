# Co-Researcher — Academic Research Suite

## Overview

PhD-level co-researcher suite for systematic reviews, critical analysis, and academic writing.

## Available Research Skills

- **academic-writing** — Academic writing patterns and conventions
- **critical-analysis** — Critical analysis of research papers and arguments
- **ethics-review** — Research ethics evaluation
- **grant-writing** — Grant proposal writing
- **hypothesis-testing** — Hypothesis formulation and testing
- **literature-review** — Literature review methodology
- **multi-source-investigation** — Multi-source research synthesis
- **peer-review** — Peer review methodology
- **qualitative-research** — Qualitative research methods
- **quantitative-analysis** — Quantitative analysis methods
- **research-manager** — Research project management
- **research-methodology** — Research methodology design
- **research-synthesis** — Research synthesis techniques
- **systematic-review** — Systematic review methodology
- **using-co-researcher** — How to use the co-researcher suite

## Research Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/analyze` | Analyze a research topic |
| `/bibliography` | Manage bibliography |
| `/ethics` | Ethics review |
| `/grant` | Grant writing |
| `/methodology` | Research methodology |
| `/research` | Research investigation |
| `/review` | Literature review |
| `/synthesize` | Research synthesis |

## When to Use

- Conducting systematic literature reviews
- Writing academic papers or theses
- Performing critical analysis of research
- Designing research methodology
- Synthesizing findings from multiple sources
- Writing grant proposals
