# High-Fidelity Infographic Design (4:3)

## 1. Canvas Dimensions (4:3)
- **Target Size**: 1600 x 1200 pixels.
- **Layout Grid**: Use a "Bento Box" or "Golden Ratio" layout to balance text and imagery.
- **Safe Zones**: Keep critical text 100px away from edges to ensure it's not cut off when shared on social media.

## 2. Visual Variations for Sales
- **Variation A: Modern Professional**
    - *Style*: Clean, flat design, bold sans-serif fonts (Montserrat/Inter).
    - *Palette*: Deep navy, crisp white, and gold accents (#D4AF37).
- **Variation B: Epic/Vintage**
    - *Style*: Parchment textures, serif fonts (Playfair/Cinzel), ornate borders.
    - *Palette*: Earth tones, deep reds, and aged gold.
- **Variation C: Dynamic Shonen**
    - *Style*: High-action anime lines, vibrant gradients, large impactful typography.
    - *Palette*: Electric blue, bright orange, and pure black shadows.

## 3. Sales Psychology
- **Hierarchy**: The "Desired End" (The Legacy) must be the largest visual element.
- **CTA**: Always include a clear call to action (e.g., "Start the Legacy - €5").
- **Clarity**: Limit to 10-15 "anchor" kings if the list of 19 is too cluttered for a 4:3 frame.
