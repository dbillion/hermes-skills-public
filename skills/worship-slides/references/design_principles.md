# Worship Slide Design Principles

## 1. Typography for Projection
- **High Contrast**: Always use white, off-white, or light gold text on dark backgrounds.
- **Font Selection**: 
    - *Serif*: For a traditional, sacred feel (e.g., Playfair Display, Cinzel).
    - *Sans-Serif*: For modern clarity (e.g., Montserrat, Inter, Lato).
- **Size**: Minimum 48pt for body text; 24pt for small attribution/labels.
- **Weight**: Bold or Semi-Bold to prevent "bleeding" into the background during projection.

## 2. Background Aesthetics (Atmospheric)
- **Primary Style**: Dark gradients or high-quality nature photography (mountains, stars, sunrises).
- **Secondary Style**: "Church Architectural" elements (stained glass textures, subtle stone textures).
- **Visual Rule**: Never use busy backgrounds that obscure the text. Apply a 40-60% black overlay to images.

## 3. Layout & Pacing
- **Rule of Four**: Maximum 4-6 lines of text per slide.
- **Centering**: Always center-align text vertically and horizontally.
- **Line Height**: Use generous line spacing (1.2 - 1.5x) to prevent lines from clumping.

## 4. High-Fidelity Highlights
- **Emphasis**: Use italics or a secondary color (Gold: #D4AF37) for keywords like "Grace," "Glory," or "Beautiful."
- **Transitions**: Keep transitions smooth (Fade) and timed with the natural breath of the song.
