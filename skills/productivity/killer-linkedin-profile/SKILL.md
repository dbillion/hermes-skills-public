# Writing a KILLER LinkedIn Profile

Source: *How to Write a KILLER LinkedIn Profile... And 18 Mistakes to Avoid* by Brenda Bernstein.
Objective: optimize a LinkedIn profile for maximum search visibility (SEO), professional branding, networking, and job/business generation.

## 🚨 The 18 Fatal Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Selling Yourself Short** — Lack of keywords and an ineffective, generic headline.
2. **Visual Neglect** — Unprofessional/missing profile photo or background image.
3. **Incomplete Profile** — Failing to reach "All-Star" status (missing industry, location, photo, education, etc.).
4. **Hidden Contact Info** — Outdated or incomplete Contact/Personal Info section.
5. **Weak Network** — Fewer than 500 (ideally 1,000+) connections.
6. **Poor Communication** — Ineffective, spammy, or inappropriate messaging.
7. **Generic Invites** — Sending default, unpersonalized connection requests.
8. **Blank/Boring Summary** — Copy-pasting a resume or failing to tell a compelling story.
9. **Weak Experience Section** — Listing duties instead of quantifiable accomplishments.
10. **Sloppy Presentation** — Inconsistent formatting, spelling, and grammar errors.
11. **Ugly Formatting** — Using default tiny bullets instead of engaging symbols (e.g., ►, |).
12. **Skimpy Skills** — Ignoring the Skills & Endorsements section.
13. **Inactivity** — Not posting updates, publishing articles, or engaging with others.
14. **Ignoring Special Sections** — Failing to use Volunteer, Projects, Publications, or Languages.
15. **One-Dimensional Profile** — No media items (links, videos, presentations) in Summary/Experience.
16. **Ignoring Jobs Tools** — Not using "Open Candidates," "Easy Apply," or company insights.
17. **No Recommendations** — Lacking social proof or having poorly written recommendations.
18. **Static Profile** — Treating LinkedIn like a set-it-and-forget-it resume.

## 🛠️ Phase 1: Profile Optimization & SEO (Nuts & Bolts)

### 1. The Headline & Keywords
- **Rule:** Do not just use your job title. Use a formula: `Target Title | Core Competencies | Unique Value Proposition (USP)`.
- **Keyword Strategy:** Extract keywords from target job descriptions. Place them in the Headline, Current Job Title, Summary, and Specialties.
- **Mobile Optimization:** Put the most critical keywords in the first 50 characters.
- **Location:** Use a zip code close to your target work area to capture local recruiter searches.

### 2. Visual Branding
- **Profile Photo:** 400x400 px (max 8MB). Professional headshot, plain background, looking at camera, smiling.
- **Background Banner:** 1584x396 px. Use Canva to create a banner reflecting your brand, industry, or contact info.

### 3. URL & Contact Info
- **Custom URL:** Edit public profile URL to `linkedin.com/in/firstname-lastname-keyword`.
- **Contact Info:** Include a dedicated email, phone number, and up to 3 relevant website links (portfolio, company site).

## ✍️ Phase 2: Content Creation & Storytelling

### 1. The Summary (About Section)
- **Length:** Up to 2,000 characters. First 200 characters are crucial (visible before "See more").
- **Structure:**
  - *Hook:* Sub-headline or compelling opening statement.
  - *Body:* Your story, top 3 accomplishments, and what makes you unique.
  - *Call to Action (CTA):* How to contact you or what you want the reader to do.
- **Tone:** First-person ("I") is generally preferred for a conversational, authentic tone.

### 2. Experience Section
- **Rule:** Treat it like a networking conversation, not a dry resume.
- **Format:** Engaging introductory paragraph followed by bullet points of *accomplishments* (action verbs + metrics).
- **Formatting:** Use Unicode symbols for bullets (►, ✦, ✔) to break up text and draw the eye.
- **Media:** Attach links, PDFs, or SlideShare presentations to relevant roles.

### 3. Skills & Endorsements
- **Limit:** Up to 50 skills. Pin the top 3 most relevant to your target audience.
- **Strategy:** Endorse others to trigger the "reciprocity" effect and get endorsed back.

### 4. Recommendations
- **Goal:** 5-10 high-quality recommendations.
- **Action:** Request from former managers, clients, or colleagues. Provide a "cheat sheet" of specific projects/traits to highlight.
- **Curation:** Hide outdated/generic ones; keep the most compelling visible.

## 🤝 Phase 3: Networking & Engagement (Playing the Game)

### 1. Connection Strategy
- **Goal:** 500+ connections (the "500+" badge is a trust signal).
- **Invitations:** *Never* send the default invite. Click "Add a note" and personalize referencing a shared interest, group, or mutual connection.
- **Alumni Tool:** Use the University Alumni tool to find and message graduates in target industries.

### 2. Activity & Thought Leadership
- **Posting:** Share industry news, personal insights, milestones. Use hashtags.
- **Publishing:** Write long-form articles (1,900-2,000 words) with subheadings and images. Articles are indexed by Google.
- **Engagement:** Comment thoughtfully on others' posts. Use @Mention to tag relevant connections.
- **Kudos:** Use the "Kudos" feature to recognize peers and stay top-of-mind.

### 3. Group Participation
- **Strategy:** Join up to 100 groups. Don't lurk; start conversations, answer questions, share valuable articles.
- **Networking Hack:** Message fellow group members without being connected. Target groups with active discussions (100+ comments).

## 💼 Phase 4: Job Search & Business Tactics

### For Job Seekers
- **Open Candidates:** Turn on in Settings to privately signal recruiters you're open to work.
- **Job Alerts:** Save searches; set up daily/weekly email alerts.
- **Referrals:** Use "Ask for a referral" on job postings to leverage 1st-degree connections.
- **Handling Unemployment:** Do NOT put "Seeking Opportunities" in headline. Use keywords. For current experience list "Consultant" (if true), "Volunteer," or "Part-Time" rather than leaving blank.

### For Businesses/Recruiters
- **Company Page:** Create and optimize "About Us" with keywords.
- **Showcase Pages:** Create sub-pages for specific products/services.
- **Employee Advocacy:** Ensure all employees have optimized, consistent profiles linking to the company page.

| Activity Update | 700 characters (140 to Twitter) |

## 🔒 Privacy & Security Best Practices
1. **Back Up Data:** Regularly export data (Settings & Privacy > How LinkedIn uses your data > Download your data) and save profile as PDF.
2. **Stealth Job Searching:** Turn off "Share profile changes with network" and "Share job changes" in Privacy before updating.
3. **Two-Step Verification:** Enable in Login & Security to prevent hacking.
4. **Email Privacy:** Consider a dedicated email for LinkedIn to avoid spam. Hide email from 2nd/3rd degree connections if necessary.

- [ ] Professional, high-quality profile photo and custom background banner uploaded.
- [ ] Custom, clean Public Profile URL created.
- [ ] Contact info (email, phone, websites) visible and accurate.
- [ ] Summary section keyword-rich, engaging, includes a Call to Action.
- [ ] Experience section highlights quantifiable accomplishments, not just duties.
- [ ] Formatting clean, consistent, uses engaging bullet symbols.
- [ ] Top 3 Skills pinned; at least 5-10 skills listed.
- [ ] At least 3-5 compelling Recommendations visible.
- [ ] Media (links, presentations, videos) attached to key Experience entries.
- [ ] Network has 500+ connections.
- [ ] Profile set to "All-Star" completeness.
- [ ] Activity feed updated regularly with valuable content.
