## Generating Variations

A strong post generator should produce 2-3 variations of the same core message using different patterns:

**Core Message:** "Context is the real bottleneck in engineering teams"

**Variation 1 (PSB):** Hook on problem, explain solution, deliver metric
**Variation 2 (DIA):** Open with data, provide insight, ask engagement question
**Variation 3 (Journey):** Personal story of discovering the problem, outcome-driven

Each hits different parts of your audience's awareness journey.

---

## Key Insight: Why These Patterns Work

The most engaging LinkedIn posts follow this meta-pattern:

1. **Make it specific** → Generic advice is ignored; specificity is magnetic
2. **Make it relative** → Abstract concepts don't convert; relatable problems do
3. **Make it valuable** → Every sentence should answer "why should I care?"
4. **Make it actionable** → End with something the reader can *think* about or *do*

Claude Cowork posts succeed because they do all four: specific tools (real-time sync), relatable problems (context switching), clear value (40% faster shipping), and actionable takeaway (think about YOUR team's context gaps).
