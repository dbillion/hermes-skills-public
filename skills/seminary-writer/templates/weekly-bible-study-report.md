# Weekly Bible Study Report Template

Use this template for PRT396 and similar seminary courses that require weekly Bible study reports with Scripture reading, application questions, and prayer walk reflections.

## Format

```
**[Student Name]**
**[Course Code]**
**PRT396 Spiritual Formation Seminar V**
**Weekly Bible study report**
**[Date]**

## Scripture Completion Status

[Brief statement confirming all assigned passages have been read]

## Prayer Walk

**Where did you go?**
[Location — be specific]

**What was your reason for choosing that area?**
[Connection to the Scripture themes or personal prayer needs]

**Did you take anyone along?**
[Yes/No — if yes, who and why]

## Open Discussion Questions

### [Passage 1 Reference]

1. [Open application question — practical, relevant to local church]
2. [Open application question — different angle on same passage]

### [Passage 2 Reference]

1. [Question]
2. [Question]

[Repeat for all assigned passages]
```

## Question Style Guidelines

- Write in simple, direct language
- Focus on practical application, not abstract theology
- Make questions relatable to people in a local church
- Avoid yes/no questions — use "how," "what," "in what ways"
- Connect the passage to real-life struggles (work-life balance, identity, community, burnout, obedience)
- Draw from the actual content of the passage, not generic applications

## Prayer Walk Guidelines

- Choose a specific, real location
- Connect the reason to the week's Scripture themes
- Be honest about whether you went alone or with someone
- Keep it brief — 2-3 sentences per answer
