# Banned AI Words and Phrases

Words and phrases that instantly flag writing as AI-generated. NEVER use these in seminary papers.

## Tier 1: Never Use (Instant AI Detection)

### Single Words
encompass, captivate, dynamic, delve, testament, elevate, embark, enhance, embrace, crucial, propel, explore, leverage, synergize, comprehensive, harness, spearhead, unlock, energize, pivotal, intricate, realm, landscape (as metaphor), tapestry, vibrant, foster, underscore, bolster, multifaceted, paramount, quintessential, groundbreaking, unprecedented, monumental, transformative, revolutionary, sophisticated, cutting-edge, innovative, proactive, seamless, intuitive, robust

### Phrases
- "digital world"
- "in the fast-paced world"
- "testament to"
- "gone are the days"
- "look no further"
- "dreamt of"
- "embark on a journey"
- "in this digital landscape"
- "a treasure trove of"
- "ever-changing"
- "ever-evolving"
- "daunting"
- "unleash"
- "mastermind"
- "in the realm of"
- "at the end of the day"
- "it is worth noting"
- "it is important to note"
- "needless to say"
- "it goes without saying"

### Transition Words (Formulaic)
Furthermore, However, First things first, Secondly, Firstly, Therefore, Additionally, In conclusion, In summary, Remember that, Moreover, Nevertheless, Nonetheless, Accordingly, Consequently, Hence, Thus

## Tier 2: Use Sparingly (Only When Natural)

Use these only when no simpler word works. Overuse = AI detection.

- utilize (use "use")
- facilitate (use "help")
- demonstrate (use "show")
- implement (use "do" or "carry out")
- subsequently (use "then")
- aforementioned (use "this" or "that")
- notwithstanding (use "despite")
- henceforth (use "from now on")
- in light of (use "because of")
- with regard to (use "about")
- in order to (use "to")
- at this point in time (use "now")
- due to the fact that (use "because")
- in the event that (use "if")
- has the ability to (use "can")
- is able to (use "can")

## Tier 3: Structural Patterns That Signal AI

These aren't single words but writing patterns that detectors flag:

### 1. Rule of Three
AI loves groups of three: "innovation, inspiration, and industry insights"
**Fix**: Break the pattern. Use two items, or four, or just one.

### 2. Negative Parallelism
"It's not just about X, it's about Y"
**Fix**: Just say what it is. Don't contrast with what it isn't.

### 3. Uniform Paragraph Length
Every paragraph exactly 4-5 sentences = AI
**Fix**: Mix it up. Some paragraphs 2 sentences, some 6-7.

### 4. Throat-Clearing Openers
"In this section, we will discuss..."
"This paper aims to explore..."
"The purpose of this research is to..."
**Fix**: Start with the actual content. No warm-up sentences.

### 5. Em Dash Overuse
AI uses em dashes (—) constantly for "punchy" writing
**Fix**: Use commas, parentheses, or restructure. Max 1-2 em dashes per paper.

### 6. Elegant Variation
"The protagonist faces many challenges. The main character must overcome..."
**Fix**: Just pick one term and use it. Repetition is human.

### 7. Vague Attributions
"Experts believe..." "Industry reports suggest..." "Observers have noted..."
**Fix**: Name the specific expert and cite them. If you can't, cut it.

### 8. Promotional Language
"The breathtaking region..." "The vibrant community..." "Stunning natural beauty"
**Fix**: Be plain. "The town in Gonder region" not "The breathtaking region"

### 9. Filler Phrases
- "In order to achieve this goal" → "To achieve this"
- "Due to the fact that it was raining" → "Because it rained"
- "At this point in time" → "Now"
- "It is important to note that the data shows" → "The data shows"
- "The system has the ability to process" → "The system can process"

### 10. Generic Positive Conclusions
"The future looks bright for the company. Exciting times lie ahead..."
**Fix**: Be specific. What exactly will happen? Cut vague optimism.

## What TO Do Instead

| Instead of... | Write... |
|---------------|----------|
| Furthermore, | And, So, But, Now |
| Additionally, | Also, Plus, And |
| In conclusion, | So, Here's the point |
| However, | But, Yet, Still |
| Moreover, | And, Also |
| Subsequently | Then, After that |
| Utilize | Use |
| Significant | Big, Key, Major |
| Complicated | Hard, Tough |
| Demonstrate | Show |
| Facilitate | Help |
| Numerous | Many, Lots of |
| Commence | Start, Begin |
| Endure | Last, Stay |
| Possess | Have |
| Sufficient | Enough |

## Quick Test

If you're unsure whether a sentence sounds AI, read it aloud. If it sounds like a press release or a corporate email, rewrite it. Seminary papers should sound like a thoughtful person explaining something to someone over coffee.

## The Simplest Rule

Use the first word that comes to mind. That's usually the human word. The fancy word you have to think about? That's usually the AI word.
